2026So Far: The Trade Machine Is Back OnIn progress · 2 weeks in · Birdgangflyeagles and Bjspjs4 both 4-0

Two weeks in, and with the league-median game counting as a second result each week, two teams haven't lost yet. Here's where things stand.

Bjspjs4 is back at it

Bjspjs4 has made all three trades of the season so far, all in Week 2. He sent Tee Higgins to Goldminers1 for Drake London, got Carnell Tate from dahlilama for RJ Harvey, then flipped Tate to MayockJr for Jacory Croskey-Merritt, all in the same week. The result so far is 4-0, from the 8th draft slot, the same one every champion has picked from.

The quiet man leads the league

Birdgangflyeagles hasn't made a single move this season. He's 4-0 and leads the league with 267 points, including a 143.60 in Week 2 that beat MayockJr by 71.68. Last year he made seven moves and went 3-11. Two years ago he made twelve and was the #1 seed. Doing nothing has now produced a 1 seed, a 6 seed, a last-place finish, and a 4-0 start.

Early trouble

Sleon1000, the two-time #1 seed, is 1-3. Rookie giJ03 is 0-4 and put up 55.74 in Week 2, the third-lowest score in league history. juleon8 is 2-2 overall but has won both head-to-head games, which keeps his record 10-game win streak alive.

2025424 Moves, One Blockbuster, and a Five Seed With a Ring🏆 deetee333 (5 seed) over dahlilama, 125.94–88.98

424 transactions. 11 trades. Two teams that looked unbeatable in October. And the title went to the guy who was 3-4 at the trade deadline.

424transactions
11trades
7trades by Bjspjs4 alone
1.02points: closest playoff exit

The champion nobody picked in October

Through seven weeks, deetee333 was 3-4 and going nowhere. Then came Week 8 and the biggest deal of the year: Kenneth Walker, Breece Hall and Zay Flowers to elysianbeer for De'Von Achane and Jordan Addison. Deetee went 5-2 the rest of the way, snuck in as the 5 seed at 8-6, and never lost again. He took the quarterfinal 150.78–149.76 over rbanuelos3, beat the 11-3 top seed in the semis, and won the final 125.94–88.98. That's eight wins in his last ten games. He also led the league with 38 free-agent pickups. Some guys draft a team. Deetee rented one.

On the other side of that trade, elysianbeer was 4-3 before it and 2-5 after it, and missed the playoffs at 6-8.

Seven trades, four wins

Bjspjs4 made seven trades, the most in the league by a mile. He drafted Bijan Robinson 4th overall, then shipped him to MayockJr in Week 9 for George Pickens and Ashton Jeanty. He traded for Travis Etienne in Week 7 and traded him away in Week 8. He was in the three-team deal that brought in Tyreek Hill in Week 2. The result was 4-10, the league's worst point differential (−277). He started 2026 at 4-0, so maybe it was all part of the plan.

6-0, Points King, runner-up

dahlilama opened 6-0 and scored 1,710 points, the most of anyone in 2025. That included a 176.08 in Week 8, the second-best score in league history. He added Etienne and Omarion Hampton from Bjspjs4 at the deadline, and cruised past MayockJr in the semis. Then he scored 88.98 in the championship. That's his lowest total since Week 13, and it came in the one week that mattered most.

The regular-season king, again

Sleon1000 started 7-0, finished 11-3, and grabbed the #1 seed for the second straight year. His one trade was an early swap of tight ends: Brock Bowers and RJ Harvey for Trey McBride and Jordan Mason. Then came the bye, a 78.10 in the semifinal loss to deetee333, and a loss in the third-place game. He's now 0-2 all-time in playoff games despite two #1 seeds.

1-5 to the 3 seed

First-year manager juleon8 made four trades in the first three weeks and started 1-5. Then he won eight straight, the best closing run of the season, and finished 9-5. After his last trade he averaged 127.7 points a game, including 167.34 in Week 14. He drew the 6 seed in round one and lost 104.00–91.80. That winning streak is still alive in the record book, because he opened 2026 2-0 in head-to-head games.

One point from the semis

rbanuelos3 made zero trades and closed the regular season on a six-game winning streak to go 9-5. Then he scored 149.76 in the quarterfinal, the second-highest playoff score in league history, and lost by 1.02 points. It's the highest losing score ever in this league.

Churn and burn

MayockJr led the league in total moves with 61. That's 3 trades, 26 waiver claims and 32 free-agent adds. He landed Bijan in Week 9, went 3-3 after, and snuck in as the 6 seed at 7-7. He knocked off the 3 seed, lost to dahlilama in the semis, then won the third-place game over Sleon1000.

The set-it-and-forget-it award

Birdgangflyeagles made seven moves all year, total. That's six waiver claims, one free-agent add, and no trades. He went 3-11 with a nine-game losing streak to close the season. He's 4-0 in 2026, so the lesson is unclear.

Quick hits

  • showmeyourtd23 made no trades and went 6-8, winning his last three to finish on a heater that didn't matter.
  • Goldminers1 traded Jordan Mason for Stefon Diggs in Week 2, made only 7 waiver claims all year, and went 6-8.
  • lxfiggy sent Terry McLaurin and Kenneth Walker out in the Week 2 three-team trade for Tony Pollard and Tetairoa McMillan, flipped McMillan for Courtland Sutton a week later, and went 5-9.

2024Thirteen Trades, a Points Record, and a Title That Went to the Trader Who Stopped🏆 sugarmaverick (4 seed) over rbanuelos3, 127.86–105.18

418 transactions, 16 trades, and one manager involved in thirteen of them. Meanwhile, the champion made his big move in Week 6 and then mostly stood still.

418transactions
16trades
13trades by Bjspjs4
1,858points: rbanuelos3, all-time record

The champion: one move, then cruise control

sugarmaverick was 2-3 after five weeks. In Week 6 he got DJ Moore and Jonathan Taylor from lxfiggy and took part in a three-team deal with dahlilama and Bjspjs4. From there he went 6-3, averaging 123.0 points a game, and slid in as the 4 seed at 8-6. Then he didn't lose again. He beat lxfiggy in the quarterfinal, beat the 1 seed Sleon1000 147.02–126.86 in the semis, and won the final 127.86–105.18. Two seasons in the league, one title, and a perfect 3-0 playoff record.

Thirteen trades

Bjspjs4 made 13 trades in 2024, more than the rest of the league's top three traders combined. Bijan Robinson alone showed up in four trades that year. Bjspjs4 got him twice and eventually sent him to jayy2690 in Week 10 for Justin Jefferson. After all that shuffling he finished 4-10. He was 1-4 before the Week 6 flurry and 3-6 after it.

The greatest regular season that didn't end in a ring

rbanuelos3 scored 1,858 points, the most in league history by 148. That includes the two highest single-game scores ever: 180.24 in Week 9 and 173.56 in Week 3. He went 9-5, beat Birdgangflyeagles in the semis, then scored 105.18 in the final. That was 27 points below his season average, at the worst possible time.

0-5 to the playoffs

lxfiggy started 0-5. Then in Week 6 he made two deals. He sent Bijan Robinson, Davante Adams and Stefon Diggs to Bjspjs4 for a package including DJ Moore and Jonathan Taylor, then flipped Moore and Taylor to sugarmaverick for Terry McLaurin and Brian Robinson. He went 8-1 the rest of the way at 126.6 points a game and made the playoffs as the 5 seed. He lost to sugarmaverick in round one, the same guy he'd helped build.

The collapse

showmeyourtd23 was 4-2 when he traded for Stefon Diggs, Raheem Mostert and Nick Chubb in Week 7. He didn't win another game. He lost his last nine, finished 4-10 in last place, and the streak carried into 2025 for a league-record 11 straight losses.

The defending champ's hangover

dahlilama won the 2023 title and made five trades in 2024. He also scored 48.42 in Week 3, still the lowest single-game score in league history, and went 6-8. He lost five straight from Week 9 to Week 13 and missed the playoffs.

Quick hits

  • Sleon1000 took the 1 seed at 10-4 and lost 147.02–126.86 to the eventual champ in the semis. He won the third-place game.
  • Birdgangflyeagles made 14 moves all year, no trades, went 8-6 as the 6 seed, and upset the 3 seed MayockJr in round one.
  • deetee333 went 8-6 and missed the playoffs. He was one of four 8-6 teams and the only one left out, on points.
  • jayy2690 played one season, went 5-9, and was on the other end of the Jefferson-for-Bijan trade.

2023Red Solo Kupp Trades Kupp, Wins It All🏆 dahlilama (3 seed) over showmeyourtd23, 141.66–118.40

The first season. 425 transactions, 14 trades, one trade that was undone the same week, and a champion who traded away the player his team was named after.

425transactions
14trades
0.66points: dahlilama's semifinal margin
62moves by deetee333

He traded his namesake and won

dahlilama's team was called Red Solo Kupp. He drafted Cooper Kupp 8th overall, and in Week 11 he traded Kupp to MayockJr for Saquon Barkley. He made four trades in all, including picking up Mike Evans in a three-team deal in Week 4. He went 9-5 as the 3 seed, beat luieloo 143.16–74.64, then beat the 2 seed Bjspjs4 in the semis 116.66–116.00. That 0.66-point margin is the closest playoff game in league history. He won the final 141.66–118.40. The league's first champion traded the player on his letterhead.

The zero-trade finalist

showmeyourtd23 didn't make a single trade all year. He went 8-6 as the 4 seed, beat deetee333 134.60–109.66, and knocked out the 1 seed Birdgangflyeagles 110.90–100.68 to reach the first title game. It's still his best finish.

The top seed who didn't touch anything

Birdgangflyeagles made 12 moves all season and no trades. He went 10-4 and took the 1 seed, then lost the semifinal to showmeyourtd23. He bounced back to score 158.84 in the third-place game, the highest score of the entire 2023 season.

The trade that happened twice

In Week 4, deetee333 and sugarmaverick swapped David Montgomery, Travis Etienne and Dameon Pierce. Then they swapped them straight back, the same week. Then both jumped into a three-team deal with dahlilama instead. sugarmaverick made 7 trades, the most that year, and went 7-7. deetee333 made 5 trades and a league-high 45 free-agent adds (62 moves total), went 8-6, and lost in the quarterfinals.

The unluckiest season on record

Sleon1000 scored 1,552 points, fifth-most in the league, and went 5-9. He had 1,675 points scored against him, the most of any team in any season. He'd go on to win the next two regular-season titles.

Quick hits

  • Bjspjs4 made 5 trades, including getting Jahmyr Gibbs from rbanuelos3 in Week 11, went 9-5 as the 2 seed, and lost the semi by 0.66. He scored 98.82 in the third-place game.
  • MayockJr was 4-3 when he traded Brandon Aiyuk and Kyren Williams for four players in Week 8, then went 2-5 and finished 6-8.
  • Goldminers1 lost eight straight after Week 1, went 4-10, and scored 57.28 in Week 4.
  • Criscutfries made eight moves all year, went 3-11, and finished last in his only season.
  • luieloo made 47 moves in his one season and snuck in as the 6 seed at 8-6.
